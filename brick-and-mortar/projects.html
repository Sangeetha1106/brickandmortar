Mudichur – 3800 Sq.Ft Apartment

📍 Mudichur, Chennai

Build Up: 3800 Sq.Ft Multi-Unit Apartment

Client’s safety & hassle-free handover were our top 2 priorities. Mudichur, Chennai – a flood-prone area, had land issues and unsatisfied service from previous builders. We solved all these problems and handed over the residential building.

Vengaivaasal – 2200 Sq.Ft Individual House

📍 Vengaivaasal, Chennai

Build Up: 2200 Sq.Ft Individual House

The client had very unique requirements: an extremely affordable house, with a blend of ultra-modern architecture, and curated room dimensions.

We constructed this individual house with mezzanine flooring, foldable furniture for space optimization, and work from home provision in the living room.
