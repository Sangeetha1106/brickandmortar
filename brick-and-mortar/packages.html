Pricing Questions

Packages & Pricing FAQs

Why are per-sq.ft rates not listed?
Cost depends on plot size, soil conditions, design and the specifications you choose. After a site visit we give you an accurate, itemised quotation instead of a generic rate.
Can I upgrade or mix features between packages?
Yes. Packages are starting points; we can customise specifications and add upgrades like interiors or a rooftop garden to suit your budget.
How are payments structured?
Payments are tied to verified construction milestones, agreed in writing before work begins.
Will you help me with the bank loan process?
Yes. We help with the documentation and plan approvals that banks require.
Is the quotation free?
Yes. Contact us to schedule a site visit and receive your itemised quotation.
