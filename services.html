Proposed Workflow

Our 5-Step Working Process

A structured approach for planning, executing, and delivering your construction project seamlessly.

1

Consultation

Initial meeting to understand your plot size, requirements, architectural style, and budget preferences.

2

Design & Planning

Architectural 2D floor plans, 3D elevation options, structural drawings, and vasthu-friendly layouts.

3

Estimation

4

Construction

5

Project Handover

Final quality inspection, deep cleaning, client walkthrough, and celebratory key handover.
