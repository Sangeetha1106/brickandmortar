Company Profile

Building With Strength, Honesty & Care Since 2010

Brick & Mortar Constructions is a premier Chennai-based construction firm founded in 2010 by Er. Sankar K., specializing in residential and commercial building construction, renovation & repair, and bespoke interiors.

Our core team consists of in-house architects, structural engineers, and interior designers. We provide complete end-to-end solutions for our clients from building design to construction and interiors, which includes hassle-free loan processing and seamless government regulatory approvals.

⚡

Resource Optimization

Our commitment to minimize wastage of time, labor, raw materials, and energy drastically reduces construction costs without compromising structural safety.

🌿

Climate-Responsive Design

Thoughtfully planned architecture that reduces power consumption for natural cooling, cross-ventilation, and daytime illumination.

  • Clear, upfront pricing with zero hidden charges
  • Milestone-based delivery with regular site progress updates
  • Tested, high-grade branded materials and daily engineering supervision
  • Vasthu-compliant architectural planning and sanction support
