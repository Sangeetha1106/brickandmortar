Stage By Stage

How Your Building Takes Shape

Every construction stage has defined checks, so you always know what is happening on your site.

1

Excavation & Foundation

Marking, excavation and footing work as per structural design, with steel and concrete checks before every pour.

2

Plinth & Ground Beam

Plinth beam, filling and compaction with damp-proofing so your home stays strong and dry for years.

3

Superstructure

Columns, brickwork and lintels raised floor by floor with plumb and alignment inspections.

4

Roof Slab & Waterproofing

Slab shuttering, reinforcement and concreting followed by proper curing and terrace waterproofing.

5

MEP & Plastering

Electrical, plumbing and plastering completed in sequence to avoid rework and wastage.

6

Finishing & Handover

Flooring, painting, fittings and a final snag-check before we hand the keys to you.

Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build on my own plot, and do you help with design?
Yes. You bring the plot and your requirements; our in-house architects prepare the plan, 3D elevation and structural drawings, and our engineers build it.
Can you help with building approvals and bank loans?
We assist with building plan sanction, municipal approvals and the documentation banks ask for, so the paperwork does not slow your project down.
How do I know the quality of materials being used?
We use tested, branded cement, Fe550-grade TMT steel and first-class bricks. Materials and each stage are inspected by our site engineers and shared with you through regular progress updates.
Will there be hidden costs during construction?
No. You receive an itemised estimate before the contract is signed, and billing follows verified milestones.
Can I get interiors and construction from the same team?
Yes. Our Turnkey and Premium offerings combine construction and interiors under one contract so you deal with a single point of contact.
